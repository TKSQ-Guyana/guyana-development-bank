1. Citizen (Applicant / Borrower)
Getting in

Sign in with e-ID (three-box number) or email and password
Create an account if the e-ID is new
Give consent — recorded with the version of the terms they agreed to
View their details: what they declared, and separately what the government records say
Applying
5. Choose how they're applying: alone, as a partnership, or through a cluster
6. Choose existing business or new venture
7. Fill the form that matches that route — business details, market, customers, operations, team, financials or projections, and what the money is for
8. Save a draft and come back to it later
9. See exactly what's still missing before submitting
10. Submit

Documents
11. Upload documents and say what each one is
12. Replace a document with a newer version
13. See which documents are still wanted
14. Open any document they've uploaded

While it's being decided
15. Track where the application has reached
16. See when the bank asks for something specific, and send exactly that back

The offer
17. Read the full letter of offer — amount, term, monthly instalment, total repayable, conditions
18. Accept it by confirming their name, or decline with a reason
19. See when the Bank has countersigned

Before the money
20. See the list of conditions that must be met first
21. Complete the ones that are theirs to do
22. See progress on the ones the bank is checking

After the money
23. Confirm they received the funds
24. View the repayment schedule — every instalment, date and balance
25. Make a payment
26. See their full payment history
27. Request a statement
28. See clearly if anything is overdue
29. Keep access to everything after the loan closes

Clusters
30. Create a cluster and invite members by e-ID
31. Accept an invitation
32. See the member list

Partnership
33. Add co-applicants by e-ID
34. Each co-applicant signs the offer themselves
35. Any partner may make a payment; all remain responsible

Cannot: see anyone else's application or documents, decide anything, or see internal bank notes.

2. Underwriter
Review queue of submitted applications, identified by e-ID not email
Open a case and see everything the applicant entered
Open and read every uploaded document
See the applicant's declared details next to the government-verified ones, with differences highlighted
Request specific missing documents or information, in words the applicant will see
Approve — for the amount asked or less, never more
Decline
Write down the reason — required, cannot be left blank
Sign and issue the letter of offer
Book the loan once the offer is accepted and countersigned
See their own decision history
Cannot: release money, verify conditions, change any lending rule, or review their own application.

3. Disbursement Officer
See loans waiting on conditions
Check each condition and mark it met or not met, with a note
Countersign the accepted offer on the Bank's behalf
See the disbursement queue — everything cleared and awaiting release
Release funds, in full or in part
Export the payment file for the bank
Record what actually happened to the payment — succeeded, failed, returned
Record a payment exception and what was done about it
Cannot: decide credit, and cannot release a loan they approved, applied for, or are connected to. The system refuses it.

4. Finance
See money that has arrived from the bank and match it to loans
Reconcile — see what's matched, what isn't, and why
View the full loan ledger
Handle refunds where someone paid more than they owed
Portfolio reporting, down to individual cases
Propose a change to any lending rule: interest rate, maximum, minimum, loan term limits, required documents, standard conditions, capacity calculation, charges
Set the date a rule change takes effect
See the history of every rule change and who approved it
Cannot: decide credit, release money, or approve their own proposal.

5. Board / CEO
Portfolio dashboard — total lent, outstanding, repaid, overdue
Disbursement and repayment trends over time
Arrears summary
Approve or reject a rule change proposed by finance
See the full history of rule changes
Cannot: see any individual applicant, application, document or case. This is enforced, not just hidden.

6. Platform Admin
Create and manage user accounts
Grant and remove roles
Disable a user — the kill switch, which works even if their e-ID still authenticates elsewhere
System health, logs, background job status
Manage integration settings and credentials
Cannot (by policy): decide credit or move money. Note: technically the admin account can still do both today — that's a known gap you chose to leave for now.

The three rules underneath all of it
The person who approves a loan is never the person who releases it
The person who approves a loan is never the person who checks its conditions
The person who proposes a rule is never the person who approves it
