WHY THIS BANK EXISTS

What the capital
is for.

A national programme, not a product on sale. It has five purposes, and all of them are about what happens outside this building: the workshops, the fields and the shopfronts that already keep the country running.
01

Building prosperity

Growth you can feel where you live and work: the shops, the fields and the workshops of every region, not only the national accounts.

02

Raising incomes

A business that can take the next job, buy the next stock and pay a better wage. Higher earnings for the owner and for the people they employ are the point of the capital, not a side effect.

03

Putting power in people's hands

Decisions about what gets built stay with the person who knows the trade. The programme supplies the capital. It does not supply the plan, or the conditions a lender usually attaches.

04

Bringing new businesses into being

Capital for a first venture, not only for one with years of audited accounts behind it. A business that never starts employs nobody, buys from nobody and trains nobody.

05

Standing behind small and medium enterprises

Most working Guyanese earn their living in a small or medium enterprise. They are the reason this programme exists, not one segment of a lending book.

THE PROPOSED TERMS

What Guyana
is offering

Per loan
G$3M

The proposed maximum for a single GDB loan. It is not a promise of approval, and the funding is not in place yet.

Interest
0%

You repay what you borrowed and nothing more. There is no arrangement fee and no cost to apply.

Security
No collateral

Land, property or family wealth is not a condition of borrowing under this programme.

Above the cap
No co-financing

Requests must stay at or below G$3M. Nothing larger can be arranged through this portal.

G$3M is a proposed ceiling, not an offer. Nobody is approved in advance, and the funding is not in place yet.

Applying is free. There is no fee at any stage, no one can move you up the queue, and anyone asking you to pay for help with your application is not acting for the Government of Guyana.

A person decides. You can be guided through the preparation and your answers can be organised for you. Submitting, approving, declining and disbursing stay with a human underwriter at every step.
