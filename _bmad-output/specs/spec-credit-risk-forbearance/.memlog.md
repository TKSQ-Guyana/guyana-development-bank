- (decision) Root cause is a missing credit-risk and forbearance capability, not three UI gaps; the scenarios are symptoms.
- (decision) Central domain decision: a schedule-change event is either a CONCESSION (forbearance, borrower in difficulty) or a BORROWER-ELECTED PREPAYMENT. lending files both under one Loan Restructure doctype; GDB must classify them apart because regulators treat them oppositely.
- (capability) CAP-1 Risk parameters are proposed, approved by a second officer, effective-dated, and APPLIED to the Loan Product and Company.
- (capability) CAP-2 The book ages: every live loan carries a days-past-due and a classification bucket derived nightly.
- (capability) CAP-3 A borrower can request a later first payment; an underwriter grants a moratorium within policy bounds; it is stated on the Letter of Offer and written onto the Loan at booking.
- (capability) CAP-4 Release is refused when the booked schedule does not carry the executed offer terms, including moratorium.
- (capability) CAP-5 Every schedule-change event is classified Concession or Borrower-elected prepayment and reported apart.
- (capability) CAP-6 A concession is proposed by one officer with a reason code, approved by a second, accepted by the borrower as a variation, then flagged with a watch period.
- (capability) CAP-7 When a payment would be applied ahead of schedule the borrower is asked how to apply it - finish sooner or pay less each month - and their instruction is recorded against the payment.
- (capability) CAP-8 Schedule-change history is readable by Underwriter, Disbursement Officer and Finance, and by the borrower for her own loan.
- (capability) CAP-9 A daily control detects any loan whose master terms disagree with its Active schedule, or any schedule change with no maker and checker.
- (capability) CAP-10 Credit discretion is bounded by a delegated lending authority matrix; beyond it the case escalates.
- (constraint) Build on frappe/lending's own doctypes and fields - Loan Restructure, moratorium_tenure/_type, advance_payment_handling, loan_classification_ranges. No second state machine, no money recomputed in the portal.
- (constraint) Every schedule version is retained; a new schedule never erases the prior one (project_overview.md:1807). lending already does this - it must stay true and become visible.
- (constraint) Maker must not equal checker on any state-changing credit or money action, enforced server-side on the record (the GDBLendingRuleProposal.before_save and disburse_loan pattern), never only in the UI.
- (constraint) Any change to terms the borrower has executed requires her acceptance in her own name before it takes effect.
- (constraint) GDB lends at 0%, so a moratorium or a prepayment reshapes cash flow and never changes total repayable; no interest capitalisation arises.
- (constraint) Risk parameters live in records and configuration, never hardcoded; changing one goes through CAP-1.
- (constraint) Whitelisted API surface only via gdb_bank; identity, roles and session handling are unchanged by this work.
- (decision) NON-GOAL: not building a replacement restructure or amortisation engine; lending computes every figure.
- (decision) NON-GOAL: not implementing IFRS 9 expected-credit-loss modelling; classification and provisioning posting only.
- (decision) NON-GOAL: not building the Board/CEO persona here, though CAP-2 and CAP-5 produce its inputs.
- (decision) NON-GOAL: not changing lending's behaviour of rescheduling on an advance payment; that behaviour is correct and stays.
- (decision) NON-GOAL: the System Manager / Administrator bypass stays a known accepted gap, unchanged by this work.
